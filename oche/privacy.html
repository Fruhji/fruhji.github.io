Dart Scorer – Privacy Policy

Summary: OCHE collects no personal data whatsoever. All scores and statistics are stored exclusively on your device. There are no servers, no cloud uploads, and no tracking.

1. General

This privacy policy applies to the iOS and Android app OCHE – Dart Scorer (referred to as "the App"). It describes what data the App processes and how it is used.

2. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No user account is created or required.
  • No communication with external servers takes place.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.
  • No device data, location data, or usage statistics are collected.

3. Local Storage

The App stores scores, player profiles, and statistics in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to you.

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the App, or on Android additionally by clearing the App's data in the system settings.

4. Internet Access

The App does not connect to the internet. On Android it does not request the internet permission.

5. Third Parties

The App uses no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services.

6. Children

The App is suitable for all age groups. Since no data is collected, there are no specific risks for children under GDPR or COPPA.

If anything changes about how data is handled, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly. The date of the last update is shown below.
