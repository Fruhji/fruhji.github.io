OCHE

Dart Scorer for Tournaments – Support

OCHE is a professional dart scorer for iOS and Android – fast, clear, and fully offline. X01, Cricket, Around the Clock, and Shanghai with detailed statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does the app need an internet connection? No. OCHE works completely offline. All data stays on your device.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is no registration and no login.
  • Where are my statistics stored? In a local database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all data.
  • Which game modes are available? X01 (301, 501, 701 and more with Double-In/Double-Out, legs and sets), Cricket, Around the Clock, and Shanghai.
  • Which languages are supported? The app is available in 27 languages, including English and German.
